================================================================================
FLAG DESCRIPTIONS FOR EDITIT WEB TOOLTIPS
================================================================================
This file contains descriptions for all flags used in EditIt.
Each description is intended for tooltip text in the GUI.

Format: CONSTANT_NAME = bit_value
  Description text...
  Source: where it's defined/used

Notes:
  - Descriptions are in English (will be translated to Italian later)
  - Flags marked [UNUSED] are defined but not functional in the code
  - Flags marked [IMMORTAL] are only visible/usable by immortals
  - Flags marked [WORN OBJ ONLY] apply only to worn object afflictions

================================================================================
SECTION 1: ACT_* - MOBILE ACTION FLAGS
================================================================================

ACT_IS_NPC = 1 (bit 0)
  Auto-set for all mobile NPCs. Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:843, used everywhere via IS_NPC() macro

ACT_SENTINEL = 2 (bit 1)
  The mobile stays in its assigned room and will not wander.
  Used during area reset to keep guards and shopkeepers in place.
  Source: merc.h:844, db.c:2087

ACT_SCAVENGER = 4 (bit 2)
  The mobile picks up valuable objects from the ground and keeps them.
  Scavengers will take the most valuable item in the room.
  Source: merc.h:845

ACT_TO_VINDICATE = 8 (bit 3)
  The players will acquire the wanted status when killing this mobile,
  and will be hunted by mobile guards.
  Source: merc.h:846, fight.c:920

ACT_HUNTER = 16 (bit 4)
  The mobile actively hunts wanted criminals (PLR_WANTED).
  Hunters check a wanted list and pursue marked players across areas.
  Source: merc.h:847, mspecial.c (spec_hunter)

ACT_AGGRESSIVE = 32 (bit 5)
  The mobile attacks players on sight without provocation.
  Aggressive mobs will initiate combat when a player enters the room.
  Source: merc.h:848, fight.c:1924

ACT_STAY_AREA = 64 (bit 6)
  The mobile will not leave its home area when chasing or wandering.
  Prevents mobs from following players into other zones.
  Source: merc.h:849, fight.c:2619

ACT_WIMPY = 128 (bit 7)
  The mobile flees when badly hurt (below 25% HP).
  Wimpy mobs will attempt to flee from combat.
  Source: merc.h:850, fight.c:997

ACT_PET = 256 (bit 8)
  Auto-set for tamed pets. Indicates the mobile is a player's companion.
  Pets follow their master and share experience. Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:851, act_obj.c:3019

ACT_TRAIN = 512 (bit 9)
  The mobile can train players in stats (strength, intelligence, etc.)
  Immortals can use the "train" command near these mobs.
  Source: merc.h:852, act_move.c:1944

ACT_PRACTICE = 1024 (bit 10)
  The mobile is a guildmaster who can teach skills/spells.
  Players can use the "practice" command near these mobs.
  Source: merc.h:853, act_info.c:2847

ACT_GAMBLE = 2048 (bit 11)
  The mobile runs a gambling game (cards, dice, etc.)
  Croupiers with this flag can interact with each other. Unused.
  Source: merc.h:854, act_move.c:2652

ACT_VINDICATIVE = 4096 (bit 12)
  The mobile actively tracks the player if they flee the combat.
  Source: merc.h:855, fight.c:1274

ACT_PEACEFUL = 8192 (bit 13)
  The mobile ignores hated races and won't attack based on racial hatred.
  Peaceful mobs are exempt from racial aggro behavior.
  Source: merc.h:856

ACT_GUARD = 16384 (bit 14)
  The mobile is a city guard. It attacks criminals (PLR_KILLER, PLR_THIEF)
  and warns players with weapons in lawful areas. Also greets hero-reputation
  players and insults villains.
  Source: merc.h:857, mspecial.c (spec_guard)

ACT_MOVED = 131072 (bit 17)
  [UNUSED] Internal flag, do not set. Originally for tracking mobile movement.
  Source: merc.h:859

ACT_SAVEMOB = 536870912 (bit 29)
  The mobile's data is saved to disk. Used for persistent NPCs that retain
  their state across reboots (e.g., shopkeepers with gold).
  Source: merc.h:861, act_wiz.c:3286

ACT_SPECIAL = 1073741824 (bit 30)
  The mobile has a special function that must be always executed,
  even if there are no players in the area (e.g. mayor).
  Source: merc.h:862

SECTION 2: AFF_* - AFFECT FLAGS
================================================================================
These flags represent active magical effects or conditions on a character.

  The character is blinded and cannot see. Reduces combat accuracy.
  Source: merc.h:869, act_info.c:571

AFF_INVISIBLE = 2 (bit 1)
  The character is invisible to normal sight. Requires detect invis to see.
  Source: merc.h:870, act_info.c:300

  The character can sense evil-aligned beings. Evil creatures glow red.
  Source: merc.h:871, act_info.c:104

  The character can see invisible creatures and objects.
  Source: merc.h:872, act_move.c:2187

AFF_DETECT_MAGIC = 16 (bit 4)
  The character can sense magical auras on objects and creatures.
  Source: merc.h:873, act_info.c:107

AFF_DETECT_HIDDEN = 32 (bit 5)
  The character can detect hidden (sneaking/hiding) creatures.
  Source: merc.h:874, act_move.c:2191

AFF_HOLD = 64 (bit 6)
  The character is held/paralyzed and cannot move or act.
  Normally set only by fight skills, use with care.
  Source: merc.h:875, act_move.c:120

AFF_SANCTUARY = 128 (bit 7)
  The character is protected by sanctuary. All damage is halved.
  Source: merc.h:876, act_info.c:319

AFF_FAERIE_FIRE = 256 (bit 8)
  The character is outlined by faerie fire and cannot hide.
  Also makes them vulnerable to additional damage.
  Source: merc.h:877, act_info.c:311

AFF_INFRARED = 512 (bit 9)
  The character has infravision and can see in the dark.
  Source: merc.h:878, act_info.c:553

AFF_CURSE = 1024 (bit 10)
  The character is cursed. Prevents recall and reduces stats.
  Source: merc.h:879, act_move.c:1859

AFF_CHANGE_SEX = 2048 (bit 11)
  The character's apparent gender is changed.
  Source: merc.h:880

AFF_POISON = 4096 (bit 12)
  The character is poisoned and takes periodic damage.
  Source: merc.h:881, act_info.c:4212

AFF_PROTECT = 8192 (bit 13)
  The character is protected from evil. Evil attackers deal less damage.
  Source: merc.h:882

AFF_POLYMORPH = 16384 (bit 14)
  The character has been polymorphed into a different form.
  Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:883

AFF_SNEAK = 32768 (bit 15)
  The character moves silently and is harder to detect.
  Sneaking characters don't generate movement messages.
  Source: merc.h:884, act_move.c:1758

AFF_HIDE = 65536 (bit 16)
  The character is hidden and cannot be seen without detect hidden.
  Attacking or moving reveals hidden characters.
  Source: merc.h:885, act_move.c:1784

AFF_SLEEP = 131072 (bit 17)
  The character is magically asleep and cannot act.
  Waking requires damage or a wake spell.
  Source: merc.h:886, act_move.c:1253

AFF_CHARM = 262144 (bit 18)
  The character is charmed and obeys the charm source.
  Charmed mobs follow their master; charmed players lose some control.
  Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:887, act_comm.c:1648

AFF_FLYING = 524288 (bit 19)
  The character is flying and can cross water/chasms.
  Also provides immunity to ground-based traps.
  Source: merc.h:888, act_move.c:248

AFF_PASS_DOOR = 1048576 (bit 20)
  The character can pass through closed doors and walls.
  Source: merc.h:889, act_move.c:207

AFF_WATERWALK = 2097152 (bit 21)
  [UNUSED] Marked as unused in source code.
  Source: merc.h:890

AFF_SUMMONED = 4194304 (bit 22)
  The mobile was summoned by the character. Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:891, act_comm.c:1859

AFF_MUTE = 8388608 (bit 23)
  The character cannot cast verbal spells or speak.
  Blocks all spellcasting that requires verbal components.
  Source: merc.h:892, merc.h:2035

AFF_GILLS = 16777216 (bit 24)
  The character can breathe underwater without drowning.
  Source: merc.h:893, act_move.c:484

AFF_VAMP_BITE = 33554432 (bit 25)
  The character has been bitten by a vampire.
  Causes periodic damage and can spread vampirism. Never set this manually.
  Source: merc.h:894

AFF_GHOUL = 67108864 (bit 26)
  The character has been turned into a ghoul, so they can't
  fight, die or wear items.
  Source: merc.h:895, act_obj.c:1878

AFF_FLAMING = 134217728 (bit 27)
  The character is wreathed in flames.
  Deals fire damage to attackers in melee combat.
  Source: merc.h:896, act_info.c:322

AFF_MORTAL_POISON = 268435456 (bit 28)
  The character is afflicted with a deadly poison.
  More severe than regular poison; can be lethal without treatment.
  Source: merc.h:897, act_info.c:4214

AFF_PARALYZED = 536870912 (bit 29)
  [UNUSED] Marked as unused in source code.
  Source: merc.h:898

AFF_PIETRIFIED = 1073741824 (bit 30)
  The character has been turned to stone (petrified).
  Completely immobilizes the target. Can be cured by 'Stone to Flesh'.
  Source: merc.h:899

================================================================================
SECTION 3: ROOM_* - ROOM FLAGS
================================================================================

ROOM_DARK = 1 (bit 0)
  The room is dark. Characters without infravision cannot see.
  A light source is needed to see in dark rooms.
  Source: merc.h:1175, handler.c:1955

ROOM_NO_MOB = 4 (bit 2)
  Mobiles (except charmed pets and guards) cannot enter this room.
  Used to protect quest NPCs or safe zones from mob intrusion.
  Source: merc.h:1177, fight.c:2618

ROOM_INDOORS = 8 (bit 3)
  The room is indoors. Weather effects don't apply.
  Source: merc.h:1178, act_obj.c:4524

ROOM_UNDERGROUND = 16 (bit 4)
  The room is underground. Similar to indoors but for caves/dungeons.
  Prevents weather effects and requires light sources.
  Prevents periodic damage to vampires.
  Source: merc.h:1179, act_obj.c:4525

ROOM_LOG = 32 (bit 5)
  All commands spoken in this room are logged to file.
  Used for monitoring player behavior in specific areas.
  Source: merc.h:1180, interp.c:3731

ROOM_NO_TELEPORT = 64 (bit 6)
  Teleport spells and abilities cannot target or arrive in this room.
  Prevents magical bypass of the room's defenses.
  Source: merc.h:1181, act_wiz2.c:4902

ROOM_FOGGY = 128 (bit 7)
  The room is filled with fog. Reduces combat accuracy and visibility.
  Attacks have a chance to miss due to obscured vision.
  Source: merc.h:1182, fight.c:1033

ROOM_NO_SUMMON = 256 (bit 8)
  Summon spells cannot bring creatures into this room.
  Prevents players from summoning help into protected areas.
  Source: merc.h:1183

ROOM_PRIVATE = 512 (bit 9)
  Only 2 characters can be in this room at once.
  Used for private meeting rooms or special encounter areas.
  Source: merc.h:1184, handler.c:1988

ROOM_SAFE = 1024 (bit 10)
  No combat is allowed in this room. PvP and flagging are disabled.
  Safe rooms are typically temples or learning areas.
  Source: merc.h:1185, fight.c:1112

ROOM_SOLITARY = 2048 (bit 11)
  Only 1 character can be in this room at once.
  Used for solo quest areas or personal chambers.
  Source: merc.h:1186, handler.c:1991

ROOM_PET_SHOP = 4096 (bit 12)
  The room functions as a pet shop. Players can buy pets here.
  The room behind (south) should contain the available pets.
  Source: merc.h:1187, act_obj.c:2956

ROOM_NO_RECALL = 8192 (bit 13)
  Word of Recall spell does not work in this room.
  Characters cannot teleport back to their temple from here.
  Source: merc.h:1188, act_move.c:1858

ROOM_CONE_OF_SILENCE = 16384 (bit 14)
  Magical silence pervades the room. No verbal spells can be cast.
  Also prevents speech between characters.
  Source: merc.h:1189, magic.c:264

ROOM_SAVE = 32768 (bit 15)
  Objects on the ground in this room are saved to disk.
  Items dropped here persist across reboots (like donation rooms).
  Source: merc.h:1190, save.c:2090

ROOM_DEATHTRAP = 65536 (bit 16)
  Characters entering this room take lethal damage.
  Anything that enters (including items) is destroyed.
  Source: merc.h:1191, act_move.c:507

ROOM_NO_COMBAT = 131072 (bit 17)
  No combat of any kind is allowed. Even spells are blocked.
  Guards will glare at criminals but cannot attack.
  Source: merc.h:1192, fight.c:229

ROOM_MINI_SAVE = 262144 (bit 18)
  Like ROOM_SAVE but only saves non-takeable objects.
  Items with ITEM_TAKE flag are not saved (to prevent clutter).
  Source: merc.h:1193, save.c:396

ROOM_THEATER = 524288 (bit 19)
  The room is a theater, dispatching all messages to other rooms
  configured in a (mandatory) camera item present in this room.
  Source: merc.h:1194, comm.c:3708

ROOM_SEATS = 1048576 (bit 20)
  The room has stadium/theater seating. Characters seated here can
  watch events unfold. Prevents some spammy conditions like players
  private conversation messages, and stops players food/thirst
  condition changes.
  Source: merc.h:1195, act_comm.c:1169

================================================================================
SECTION 4: EX_* - EXIT FLAGS
================================================================================

EX_ISDOOR = 1 (bit 0)
  The exit is a door. Required for any door-related behavior.
  Without this flag, the exit is just an open passage.
  Source: merc.h:1214

EX_CLOSED = 2 (bit 1)
  The door is closed. Characters cannot pass through.
  Can be opened with the "open" command or by bashing.
  Source: merc.h:1215

EX_LOCKED = 4 (bit 2)
  The door is locked. Requires a key or lockpick to open.
  Characters cannot open it without the correct key or skill.
  Source: merc.h:1216

EX_BASHED = 8 (bit 3)
  The door has been bashed open by force.
  It remains open until repaired by a repairman NPC.
  Source: merc.h:1217, mspecial.c (spec_repairman)

EX_BASHPROOF = 16 (bit 4)
  The door cannot be bashed open. Resists all brute force attempts.
  Only lockpicking or keys can open this door.
  Source: merc.h:1218

EX_PICKPROOF = 32 (bit 5)
  The door cannot be picked with lockpicks.
  Only the correct key can open this door.
  Source: merc.h:1219

EX_PASSPROOF = 64 (bit 6)
  The door cannot be passed through magically.
  Pass Door spell and similar effects do not work on this door.
  Source: merc.h:1220

EX_HIDDEN = 128 (bit 7)
  The exit is hidden and not visible in room descriptions.
  Characters must find it through searching or special means.
  Source: merc.h:1221

EX_NOUN_MALE = 256 (bit 8)
  The door uses masculine Italian noun forms.
  Affects article usage in Italian (il/la, un/una).
  Source: merc.h:1222

EX_WINDOW = 512 (bit 9)
  The exit is a window, not a door.
  Cannot be bashed or locked like a regular door.
  Has a special watch through mechanic.
  Source: merc.h:1223

EX_HEAVY = 1024 (bit 10)
  [UNUSED] The door is heavier and harder to bash.
  Marked as not yet implemented in source.
  Source: merc.h:1224

EX_COMPLEX = 2048 (bit 11)
  [UNUSED] The door has a complex lock mechanism.
  Marked as not yet implemented in source.
  Source: merc.h:1225

================================================================================
SECTION 7: MOBILE SPECIAL FUNCTIONS (mobSpecFuncs)
================================================================================

spec_breath_any
  Dragon breath attack. Randomly selects one breath type (fire, frost,
  acid, gas, or lightning) when fighting.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_breath_acid
  Dragon breathes acid. Deals acid damage to the target.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_breath_fire
  Dragon breathes fire. Deals fire damage to the target.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_breath_frost
  Dragon breathes frost. Deals cold damage to the target.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_breath_gas
  Dragon breathes poisonous gas. Area-of-effect damage.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_breath_lightning
  Dragon breathes lightning. Deals electrical damage to the target.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_adept
  The mob casts helpful spells on low-level players (armor, bless,
  cure light, cure blindness, cure poison, refresh, combat mind).
  Only targets players below level 5.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_cleric
  The mob casts cleric spells. Heals self when hurt, buffs when idle,
  and attacks with offensive spells (blindness, faerie fire, cause
  critical, earthquake, curse, flamestrike, harm, dispel magic).
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_ghost
  The mob casts undead spells but is destroyed by sunlight.
  Uses curse, weaken, chill touch, blindness, poison, energy drain,
  harm, teleport, and summon.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_judge
  The mob casts "high explosive" on its target when fighting.
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_mage
  The mob casts mage spells. Buffs self (armor, giant strength,
  stone skin) and attacks with offensive magic (blindness, chill
  touch, weaken, teleport, colour spray, energy drain, fireball,
  polymorph other, acid blast).
  Source: mspecial.c

spec_cast_psionicist
  The mob uses psionic powers. Heals with cell adjustment and
  psychic healing, buffs with mental barrier and combat mind,
  and attacks with mind thrust, psychic drain, ego whip, psionic
  blast, ultrablast.
  Source: mspecial.c
